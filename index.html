Advancing the Science of Experience-Related Neuroplasticity

The ERN Institute is an independent research, publishing, and consulting initiative studying how life experiences—including language use, education, training, health events, and social environments—reshape brain function, cognition, and behavior across the lifespan.

Our work combines behavioral science, EEG/MRI, and advanced quantitative methods to understand how experience builds resilience, cognitive reserve, and adaptive change, while supporting the dissemination and application of rigorous research through ERN Press and ERN Consulting.
