# ERN Institute

The Experience-Related Neuroplasticity Institute is an independent research, publishing, and consulting initiative focused on how life experience shapes brain function, cognition, and behavior across the lifespan.

The website includes the Institute’s research program, leadership, consulting services, selected publications, and the Citations Per Year article-level citation framework and calculator.

ERN Press is the Institute’s scholarly publishing arm.
