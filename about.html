About ERN Institute

Why ERN exists

Experience changes the brain, but its effects are often studied in isolation: language experience in one literature, education in another, aging in another, and health or training elsewhere.

ERN Institute was created around a broader premise. These are different instances of the same fundamental scientific problem: How does accumulated experience reshape neural and cognitive systems over time, and why do individuals respond differently to the experiences they encounter?

As an independent research institute, ERN brings together mechanistic research, measurement, quantitative methods, scholarly publishing, and applied work around that question. Our goal is not simply to document that experience matters, but to understand when, how, and for whom experience-related neuroplasticity emerges.
