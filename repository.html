Transparent provenance

What ERN changed and what it did not

ERN-ready files preserve the source-reported observations or effect estimates. Preparation includes schema mapping, stable identifiers, documented effect-size conversions, and, for two examples, explicitly selected independent-study subsets. Each selection and calculation is described in the downloadable README.

The collection is redistributed under GPL-2.0-or-later, matching the metadat source package. Cite both metadat and the original publication listed on each source page.
