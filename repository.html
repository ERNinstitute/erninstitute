Transparent provenance

What ERN changed and what it did not

ERN-ready files retain the source observations. Preparation is limited to schema mapping, stable effect identifiers, compatible group totals where required, and explanatory notes. The Studio may produce different results from a publication when the original analysis used another effect-size measure, model, estimator, continuity correction, or inference method.

The launch collection is redistributed under GPL-2.0-or-later, matching the metadat source package. Cite both metadat and the original publication listed on each source page.
